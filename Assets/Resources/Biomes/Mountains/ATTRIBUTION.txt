Greenwood mountain ring (SM_mountain_ring, T_mountain_ring_*)

The terrain is real: the northern Black Forest around the Acher valley, seen from
Seebach below the Mummelsee and the Hornisgrinde on the Black Forest High Road
(Schwarzwaldhochstrasse, B500), Baden-Wuerttemberg, Germany. Generated by
Tools/Terrain/build_black_forest_ring.py.

Elevation data: AWS Terrain Tiles (Tilezen / Mapzen), which for this area are derived
from EU-DEM and SRTM. Attribution as requested by the tile provider:
  - EU-DEM: produced using Copernicus data and information funded by the European Union.
  - SRTM: NASA / USGS, public domain.
  See https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md
