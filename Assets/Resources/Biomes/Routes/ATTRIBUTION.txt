Greenwood's road and roadside follow the Schwarzwaldhochstrasse (B500),
Baden-Baden to Freudenstadt. Built by Tools/Terrain/build_b500_road.py from:

- Land cover: ESA WorldCover 10 m 2021 v200. (c) ESA WorldCover project 2021 /
  Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (2021) processed by ESA WorldCover
  consortium. Licensed CC BY 4.0.
- Elevation: AWS Terrain Tiles (EU-DEM, Copernicus; SRTM, NASA).
- Road centreline: a Google My Maps directions export (Tools/Terrain/b500.kml).
  For prototyping only: rebuild from an OpenStreetMap export (ODbL, needs
  "(c) OpenStreetMap contributors" in the credits) before release.
